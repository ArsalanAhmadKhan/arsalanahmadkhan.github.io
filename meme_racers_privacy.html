Meme Racers · Android & iOS game

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026 · Developer: Arsalan Ahmad Khan, Lahore, Pakistan

The short version

  • Meme Racers has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your coins, upgrades and unlocked racers and tracks are saved only on your own device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Who we are

Meme Racers ("the game") is developed and published by Arsalan Ahmad Khan, an independent developer based in Lahore, Pakistan. In this policy, "we" means the developer and "you" means anyone who plays the game.

Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. The game does not have sign-in, chat, leaderboards or any server of our own.

To remember your progress, the game stores the following on your device only: coin balance, upgrades, track gear, which racers and tracks you have unlocked, your selected racer and track, the sound setting, and timers that limit how often reward ads can be watched. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, optional rewarded video ads (for coins or double race rewards), and occasional full-screen ads between races. To serve, personalise, measure and protect these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share data such as:

DataWhy AdMob uses it
Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking)Show and measure ads, limit repeats, prevent fraud
IP address and approximate location derived from itShow ads relevant to your region, prevent fraud
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Show ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (views, taps, ad load errors)Measure ad performance, analytics, fraud prevention
Crash and diagnostic data from the ads SDKKeep the ads SDK working

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested, and you can choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • Android: you can reset your Advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (Settings > Privacy > Ads on some phones).
  • iOS: you can control tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete all of it by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data in your phone's settings. We hold no copy of it. To manage data held by Google for ads, use the Google settings above or visit My Ad Center.

Children

Meme Racers is a general-audience game intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared their data with us, contact us and we will help.

Security

We do not run servers or keep databases of player data. Data handled by Google AdMob is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
